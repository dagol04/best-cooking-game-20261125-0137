**Sizzle Station — Kitchen Rush** is an original fast-paced cooking game. You run the busiest little kitchen in town: take ingredients from the pantry, chop them on the board, grill patties, boil noodles, build each order on the plate, and ring the service bell before the customers lose patience. Orders keep coming, the pace quickens as the shift goes on, and a lost order costs a life. Three lives, one 150-second shift, combo bonuses for serving dish after dish without a miss.

Built from scratch with vanilla JavaScript and canvas — no libraries, no network requests, no copied art or names. Every ingredient is drawn with vector shapes, every sound is synthesized in code.

## Run it

```sh
# serve the game folder with any static server, then open it in desktop Chrome:
python3 -m http.server 8123 --directory game
# open http://localhost:8123/
```

Or just open `game/index.html` directly in a desktop browser. Play with mouse or keyboard (keys 1-6 take pantry ingredients, Space serves, Backspace clears the plate, P pauses, M mutes, R restarts).

Screenshots live in `screenshots/` — real renders of the game: the title screen, a busy kitchen with a dish mid-assembly, a plate ready to serve, and the end of a shift.

## How to play

1. Click a pantry ingredient (or press 1-6) to take it into your hand.
2. Raw food needs a station: tomato and lettuce go on the **Chopping Board**, patties on the **Grill**, noodles in the **Boiling Pot**. Bun and cheese are ready as-is — set them on the counter.
3. Finished food slides onto the **Counter**. Click a counter item to add it to the **Plate**; click the plate to take the top item off.
4. When the plate matches an order on the **Order Board**, ring the **Service Bell** (or press Space) to serve it. Patience left on the order becomes bonus points, and serving in a row builds your combo.
5. An order that runs out of patience walks out and costs a life. Survive the 150-second shift!
